# Executive Sales & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel to monitor and analyze business sales performance and profitability.
The dashboard provides insights into revenue, gross profit, gross profit margin, units sold, orders, and Cost of Goods Sold (COGS).
It helps management understand sales trends, identify profitable regions, and evaluate sales channel performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel Slicers
- KPI Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Geographical Map Charts

## Dashboard Features
- Monthly Revenue and Gross Profit Analysis
- Top Five Countries by Revenue
- Revenue Share by Sales Channel
- Region-wise Revenue Comparison
- Country-wise Geographical Analysis
- Interactive Product Category Filters
- Country and Quarter Slicers

## Business Objectives
- Monitor overall sales and profitability.
- Identify monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Compare sales performance across countries.
- Understand revenue contribution by sales channel.
- Evaluate geographical and regional performance.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Insights
- The dashboard reports a gross profit margin of 45.94%.
- Monthly revenue and gross profit trends can be compared to identify performance changes.
- Country-wise analysis helps identify major revenue-contributing markets.
- Channel-wise analysis highlights differences in revenue contribution.
- Interactive filters enable focused analysis by product category, country, and quarter.

## How to Use
1. Download the Excel dashboard.
2. Open it using Microsoft Excel.
3. Navigate through the dashboard.
4. Use the slicers to filter product categories, countries, and quarters.
5. Explore the charts and KPIs to understand business performance.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates the application of Excel-based data analytics and visualization techniques to transform sales data into meaningful business insights and support management decision-making.
